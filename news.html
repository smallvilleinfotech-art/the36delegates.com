★ FEATURED STORY MAY 2026

Kuje Prepares To Host
The 36 Innovative Delegates

Excitement continues to build as Kuje prepares to host The 36 Innovative Delegates — a premium AI, Data Analytics and Innovation discovery program for exceptional secondary school students in Abuja.

The initiative, convened by Stephen Olabamiji alongside a team of visionary innovators, is designed to expose students to analytical intelligence, innovation systems and future-focused thinking.

Only 36 students will be selected across Abuja. One student per school — making this one of the most exclusive youth programmes in the FCT.

Applications are currently open. Slots are strictly limited and will close once all schools are filled.

OUR STORY APRIL 2026

Why We Started The 36

Nigeria has no shortage of brilliant young minds. What it lacks is a structured, elite environment that treats secondary school students as future analysts and innovators — not just exam-takers.

The 36 was founded on a single conviction: if you give exceptional students the right exposure early enough, the trajectory of their lives changes forever. Stephen Olabamiji and a team of innovators built this programme to be that turning point.

One student per school. Thirty-six futures. One cohort that will define a generation.

ANNOUNCEMENT MAY 2026

Applications Now Open — Slots Are Strictly Limited

We are officially accepting applications for The 36 Innovative Delegates — Discovery Cohort 2026. The programme runs June 15–21, 2026 in Abuja, Nigeria.

Only 36 students will be admitted — one representative from each qualifying school across the FCT. Each delegate will be immersed in AI & Data Analytics, Trend Prediction, Pattern Recognition, Innovation Thinking, and an exclusive Cohort Community.

Applications close once all school slots are filled. Do not wait.
